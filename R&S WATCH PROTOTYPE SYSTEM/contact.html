About R&S Watch Restoration

For over 30 years, R&S Watch Restoration & Repair has been trusted by collectors and enthusiasts alike. Our certified watchmakers combine traditional craftsmanship with modern precision to restore and repair fine timepieces of every brand and era.

Whether it's a simple battery change or a full movement overhaul, we treat every watch with the care and respect it deserves.
